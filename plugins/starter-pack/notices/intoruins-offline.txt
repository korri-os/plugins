The original offline title loads "intoruins_main" and the main cart returns to
"intoruins". Keep both files in the same cartridge directory and start the
intoruins.p8.png title cartridge. Do not rename these two files.

These are the author's offline variants, not Korri edits. The BBS cartridges
use # identifiers that fetch BBS carts. Their names and bytes are different.
The BBS publisher's source citation and license metadata are retained in intoruins-7.txt.
