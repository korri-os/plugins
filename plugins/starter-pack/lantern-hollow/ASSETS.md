# Lantern Hollow assets

Every asset inside `lantern-hollow.qst` is either original to this quest or
comes from one of the packs below. All four packs are dedicated to the public
domain under CC0 1.0. None requires attribution; the credits are given anyway.
The archives were downloaded on 2026-10-17; `source/RECEIPTS.txt` lists the
exact URLs and SHA-256 digests.

| Pack | Creator | License page | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Tiny Town 1.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town) | Kenney (kenney.nl) | [kenney.nl/support](https://kenney.nl/support), pack `License.txt` (CC0) | Village ground, trees, houses, fences, stones, sign, key, coin |
| [Tiny Dungeon](https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-dungeon) | Kenney (kenney.nl) | Pack `License.txt` (CC0) | Dungeon floor, walls, torches, doors, hero, elder, enemies, sword |
| [15 Melodic RPG Chiptunes](https://opengameart.org/content/15-melodic-rpg-chiptunes) | Aureolus_Omicron | Listing: "CC0. No credit required." | Embedded MIDI: `rpgchip03_town`, `rpgchip06_dungeon`, `rpgchip12_the_evil_one` |
| [512 Sound Effects (8-bit style)](https://opengameart.org/content/512-sound-effects-8-bit-style) | Juhani Junkala (SubspaceAudio) | Listing CC0; pack `INFO.txt`: "released under CC0" | Ten embedded sound effects (sword, hits, pickup, fanfare, secret, door, stairs, text) |

Original to this quest (also CC0): the heart, half heart, empty heart, key
icon and menu cursor minitiles, the lantern, the heart items, the message box,
the bobbing animation frames, all maps and room layouts, all text, and the
recipe and tool source in `source/`.

Kenney tiles were composited onto Kenney ground tiles, recoloured into 16-colour
palette sets, and the hero sword was rotated. These are edits of CC0 works.

Sound effects not listed above come from the public ZQuest Classic player's own
original generated cues. The quest does not embed them.
